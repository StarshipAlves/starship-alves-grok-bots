# Email Detective

A Grok Bot that keeps your Gmail tidy and works toward inbox zero, while always asking you before it acts.

## What it does
- Once a week, reviews the past week's inbox
- Applies helpful labels (Bills, Receipts, Personal, Work, Newsletters, Promotions, and so on)
- Flags likely spam, phishing, and unwanted marketing
- For each flagged sender, asks you: **send to spam, unsubscribe, or keep in inbox**
- Remembers your answers so it doesn't ask about the same sender twice

## Files
- [`instructions.md`](instructions.md): the bot's full instructions. Paste this as the description of a new Grok Bot
- [`first-task.md`](first-task.md): the first message to send it
- [`routine.md`](routine.md): the weekly schedule

## Safety rules
- Never moves mail to spam, unsubscribes, deletes, replies, or clicks links without your explicit answer
- Asks before touching health, bank, billing, or government mail
- Warns you before any large or long-running job
